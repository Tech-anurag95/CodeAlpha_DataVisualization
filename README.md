## 🎯 1. Project Objective & Narrative
This project converts raw operational transactions into visual intelligence stories. The objective is to design executive-level dashboards and charts that enable rapid decision-making across commercial growth, fleet logistics, peak demand planning, and payment optimization.

---

## 📊 2. Visual Stories & Business Impact

| Story / Visualization | Chart Type | Key Takeaway | Business Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Regional Revenue vs. Duration** | Dual Horizontal Bar | Top cities generate highest revenue but encounter prolonged transit times. | Expand regional fulfillment hubs in congested high-revenue cities. |
| **2. Demand Intensity Matrix** | Heatmap (Day × Hour) | Order spikes occur sharply during lunch (12–15:00) and dinner (19–22:00). | Pre-schedule rider shifts to align with forecasted demand surges. |
| **3. Fleet Traffic Resilience** | Grouped Clustered Bar | Motorbikes maintain optimal speed during High Traffic; Cars experience delays. | Allocate high-density short-distance orders to Motorbike couriers. |
| **4. Payment Share & Basket Size** | Donut + Bar Chart | Wallet and Credit Card users exhibit larger average basket sizes than cash users. | Incentivize digital payment adoption via loyalty rewards. |
| **5. C-Suite Master BI Dashboard** | 6-Panel Composite | Full visibility into orders, fulfillment rates, and delivery efficiency. | Continuous executive KPI tracking. |
